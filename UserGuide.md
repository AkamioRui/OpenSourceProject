# 📘 OpenSourceProject User Guide

SIngup login loogout

Signup:

1. Click the profile picture on the right side of the Navbar
2. Click signup on the bottom of the popup
3. Sign up with your email, username, and password

Login:

1. Click the profile picture on the right side of the Navbar
2. Click signup on the bottom of the popup
3. CLick the "already have an account?"
4. Proceed to sign in with your email or username, and password

Logout:

1. Click the profile picture on the right side of the Navbar
2. Click "Logout" on the bottom of the popup

How to create a forum ~

1. Click on the "+" button at the bottom of the homepage
2. Fill in Forum title & Description
3. Choose image to upload for Forum icon & banner
4. Click "Post" button to publish forum

How to create a post in a forum ~

1. Enter a forum
2. Click on the "+" button at the bottom of forum page
3. Fill in post title & description
4. Choose image to upload for post (optional)
5. Click "Post" button to publish post

How to comment on a post ~

1. Enter a post
2. Click on the "+" button at the bottom of post page
3. Fill in the comment box
4. Click "Comment" to publish comment
